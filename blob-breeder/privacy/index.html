Cozy Blob Breeder Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026 · Luck3yApps (Cody Weisenberger)

This policy explains what information Cozy Blob Breeder ("the app") uses, why, and the choices you have. The app has no accounts and we don't run servers that receive your data. The only personal data that leaves your device goes to Google, which serves the app's ads, and to Apple or Google when you make a purchase.

Information stored on your device

Your creatures, eggs, currency, progress, settings and purchase records are saved in a local database on your device. This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Reminders, such as "your egg is ready", are scheduled on your device as local notifications. No notification server is involved.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows full-screen ads at natural breaks, served by Google AdMob. To serve, measure and protect ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android) and app-specific identifiers
  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown and tapped
  • App performance and crash information, such as launch time

Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and handles it under the Google Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

Ads are limited to content rated PG or lower.

Your choices

  • Europe (EEA), UK and Switzerland: the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can accept, decline, or manage options, and change your choice anytime from Shop > Privacy choices.
  • iPhone and iPad: ads are personalized only if you allow tracking when asked. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Google or privacy settings.
  • Remove Ads: a one-time purchase in the Shop turns ads off. After that, the app stops requesting ads and doesn't start Google's ads SDK.

Purchases

In-app purchases (gold packs and Remove Ads) are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app receives only a confirmation and transaction ID, which it uses on your device to deliver your purchase and avoid double-crediting it.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we'll help.

Data retention and deletion

We don't store your data on our side, so there is nothing for us to delete. Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the app. Google retains ad data under its own policies; you can manage it through your device settings and Google's ad controls.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
